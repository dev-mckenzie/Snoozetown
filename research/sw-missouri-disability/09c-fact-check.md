## Summary
Counts: 36 confirmed rows, 8 corrections, 3 could not verify, 5 cross-file notes (2 real conflicts), plus 6 wording cautions.

Most serious corrections first:
1. **The 2027 power map is out of date: 11 named legislators (8 House, 3 Senate) cannot be in their seats in 2027.** Fogle (HD 135, Budget ranking member) and Stinnett (HD 133, Health and Mental Health chair) are both running for the open SD 30 seat, so they leave the House. John Black (HD 129, HMHSS vice chair), Kelley, Roberts, Bromley, Knight and Cupps are term-limited. In the Senate, Crawford (SD 28) and Brown (SD 16), both Appropriations members, are term-limited, and so is Hough (SD 30). Senate Appropriations Chair Rusty Black (SD 12) and Trent (SD 20, who faced a primary challenger) are on the ballot. Primary results could not be confirmed (SOS sites blocked).
2. **The $80 million add-back is mischaracterized.** News reports tie it to reversing proposed cuts to self-directed supports AND day habilitation (Missouri Independent: $80.7 million total), not self-directed supports alone. No primary source; keep it labeled news only.
3. **Regional DD counts are public.** The FY27 DMH Program Descriptions Book, p. 123, gives FY2025 caseload: Springfield RO 4,627 and Joplin Satellite 2,692. File 02 says none exist.
4. **05 says Missouri DSP wage and turnover data were not found.** File 02 has them and they check out (NCI-IDD 2024: turnover 46.5%, wage $18.07, 28.0% of agencies turned away referrals).
5. Minor page cites: Sec. 10.3005 is on p. 59 only; the $95,815,326 supplemental note is on Executive Budget p. 148.

All core budget and bill claims in 05 are confirmed from primary sources: HB 2010 Secs. 10.2015 (127 slots), 10.3005, 10.2020 ($33.00 an hour), 10.410; HB 2372 (signed 13 Jul 2026, effective 28 Aug 2026); HB 3467 (16 to 0, 11 to 0, never reached the floor); 6 p.m. Friday 7 May 2027 budget deadline; 6 Jan 2027 session start; the 15 Oct 2026 DDD memo. All key 02 numbers checked are confirmed (several recomputed from Census data).
