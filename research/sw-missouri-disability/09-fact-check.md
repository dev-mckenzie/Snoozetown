## Summary
Counts (table rows; many Confirmed rows group several facts from one source): **8 corrected, 8 could not verify (one row lists about 18 items not attempted), 53 confirmed, 6 contradictions between files.** Sources are primary unless marked secondary. Nothing in the three files was invented; most errors come from official sources that disagree with each other.

Most serious first:
1. **Freeze exceptions (High, could not verify).** The 15 Oct 2026 memo is confirmed word for word (Comprehensive, Community Support, PfH; budget; no end date; current participants keep services; only Inter-Divisional Agreement and federally mandated cases continue; MOCDD as usual). It names **no crisis, PON 5 or emergency exception**. 9 CSR 45-2.017(5)(C) and (6) give emergency cases top place on the list, not a slot. No guide or screener should say a crisis gets a person onto a waiver now.
2. **Adult DD functional test (01, corrected).** 01 says an adult can qualify with ONE area. Wrong: the sheet and 9 CSR 45-2.010(3)(C) require TWO areas for adults. Fix the 01 screener rule.
3. **Office addresses (01, 03, 04, corrected).** Springfield street ZIP is 65802 (mail: PO Box 5030, 65801-5030); DDD's 65810 is wrong. Joplin street ZIP is 64801 per the state facility record; DMH prints the PO-box ZIP 64802. All phone and fax numbers are right.
4. **Attorney notice in DD eligibility appeals (01, corrected).** Rule: 10 calendar days after counsel is retained; DDD sheet says 10 business days. Use the shorter period.
5. **Eligibility clock (contradiction).** DDD's sheet gives itself 60 business days for the MAAS after records arrive; the rule allows 30 (+5) business days. The rule controls.
6. **County boards vs PfH map (could not verify).** All 10 "no SB40 board" counties in 01 are PfH counties on the DDD map. Check before the screener says "no county fund".
7. Case law in 04: all names, courts, years and citations check out. Fix the Lankford summary (comparability claim failed), the Link quotation, and the word "common" for Crocker.
