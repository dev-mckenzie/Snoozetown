# Advocacy Toolkit: Disability Services in Southwest Missouri

Date: 27 October 2026. For the client's own use. Companion to the policy report (10-policy-report.md).

This toolkit holds talking points for each audience and three draft templates. All drafts use [placeholders]. Nothing here has been sent. It is not legal advice; check nonprofit lobbying rules (Section 6) before you use it.

## 1. Rules for using these facts

These rules come from the three fact checks. Follow them in every letter, comment, and testimony.

1. **Do not name legislators yet.** 11 legislators named in our research (8 House, 3 Senate) cannot hold their current seats in 2027. Committee posts change after the 3 November 2026 election and the 6 January 2027 session start. Address letters by role ("Chair, House Budget Committee") or look up the person in January.
2. **Do not say a crisis gets a person onto a waiver now.** The 15 October 2026 memo names no crisis or emergency exception. Emergency cases (PON 5) get top place on the wait list, not a slot. Say: "The memo does not say emergency cases are exempt. Ask the Regional Office Director in writing."
3. **Quote Section 10.2015 exactly.** It says: "No funds shall be expended in furtherance of waiver slots assigned on July 1, 2026 except for" 127 named slots (50 crisis residential, 27 nursing home transition, 39 Children's Division transition, 11 MOCDD transition). Do not add the word "beyond."
4. **Label the $80.7 million.** Say: "about $80.7 million (all funds) to reverse proposed cuts to DD self-directed supports and day habilitation, according to news reports; no primary budget document gives the figure." Do not quote a proposed hourly rate or cut sizes from news; they could not be confirmed.
5. **Regional DD counts are public; wait list counts are not.** On 30 June 2025, the Springfield Regional Office served 4,627 people and the Joplin Satellite Office 2,692 (FY27 DMH Program Descriptions Book, p. 123). Do not estimate the number on the wait list. Use only DMH figures or your own counted cases.
6. **Missouri DSP data exist.** NCI-IDD 2024: turnover 46.5%, average wage $18.07 an hour, 28.0% of agencies turned away referrals for lack of staff.
7. **Do not say "this county has no county DD fund"** for Barton, Cedar, Dade, Dallas, Henry, Hickory, McDonald, Ozark, St. Clair, or Vernon until the county clerk confirms it. The 2020 association map and the DMH map disagree.
8. **Use the corrected office details.** Springfield Regional Office: 1515 East Pythian Street, Springfield, MO 65802 (mail: PO Box 5030, Springfield, MO 65801-5030), 417-895-7400. Joplin Satellite Office: 3600 East Newman Road, Joplin, MO 64801, 417-629-3020.
9. **Share family stories only with written consent.** Use first names only if the family agrees.

## 2. Talking points

Each point gives its source in brackets. Full links are in Section 6.

### A. State lawmakers and the budget committees

1. Since 15 October 2026, new enrollment in the Comprehensive, Community Support, and Partnership for Hope waivers is on a wait list "due to current budget limitations," with "no estimated end date." [Memo]
2. The FY2027 budget law limits new waiver slots to 127 named exceptions (HB 2010, Section 10.2015). Only the General Assembly can change this, through a FY2027 supplemental or the FY2028 House Bill 10. [HB 2010, p. 57]
3. You already receive the count. Section 10.3005 requires a monthly report to the House Budget and Senate Appropriations chairs and ranking members on "individuals presenting for service that have not been assigned a waiver slot," with the cost to serve them. Please make it public by county and Regional Office. [HB 2010, p. 59]
4. Fund the base budget, not only patches. Supplementals were needed in March 2025 and March 2026. County DD boards say "Reliance on supplemental funding creates gaps, delays, and costly inefficiencies." [Senate Governor action lists; MACDDS]
5. A funded slot needs a worker. In 2024, Missouri DSP turnover was 46.5%, the average wage was $18.07 an hour, and 28.0% of agencies turned away referrals. FY2027 law holds DD rates at the 1 January 2026 level and home care rates at the 1 January 2025 level. [NCI-IDD 2024; HB 2010 Sections 10.2020, 10.2030]
6. School leavers face two closed doors. VR Categories II and III have been closed since 9 December 2024, and the waiver that pays for long-term job support is now wait-listed. Please fund the VR state match and a set of slots for school leavers with a VR job plan. [VR State Plan; Memo]
7. Local money is already in the state budget. FY2027 counts on $5,889,538 of county SB40 tax money as Medicaid match. Protect SB40 levies in any property tax change, and pass an option like HCS HB 3467, which cleared two House committees 16 to 0 and 11 to 0 in 2026. [HB 2010; HB 3467 summary]
8. Work must not cost health coverage. Missouri's Medicaid buy-in for workers lapsed on 28 August 2025 and returned on 28 August 2026 (HB 2372). Please guard against another lapse. [HB 2372 summary]
9. Local fact: [number] people I work with in [county] are waiting for a waiver. (Use your own counted cases or a DMH figure. Do not estimate.)

### B. State agencies (DMH, DSS and FSD, MO HealthNet, DHSS, DESE and VR)

Comments to agencies on rules and rates are not lobbying under the federal 501(h) test.

1. DMH: please post monthly wait list counts by waiver, PON, Regional Office, and county. The page sections already exist but show no numbers. [DMH wait list page]
2. DMH: please state in writing whether emergency (PON 5) cases can enroll during the wait list, and treat predictable loss of a caregiver (age, serious illness) as a priority factor. [Memo; 9 CSR 45-2]
3. DMH and VR: please agree in writing whether a closed VR category counts as VR "not available" for waiver employment services. [DD Waiver Manual; VR State Plan]
4. MO HealthNet: at 21, outpatient therapy, private duty nursing, and several devices stop or move to the exceptions process. Psychological testing is capped at 4 hours per provider per year. Please review adult coverage for people with lifelong disabilities. [MO HealthNet manuals]
5. MO HealthNet: please publish CDS and personal care vendor counts by county before extending the 1 August 2026 enrollment moratorium. [MMAC notice]
6. DSS and FSD: please publish the eligibility manuals in plain HTML and correct the SNAP chart, which still shows FY2026 limits (FY2027 for 1 person: $1,729 gross, $1,330 net a month). [USDA FNS; DSS chart]
7. DHSS: please apply for the next federal Lifespan Respite grant, and tell families in writing that a legal guardian cannot be the paid CDS attendant. [ARCH; HCBS Manual 3.25]
8. DESE: please state whether a student who turns 21 may finish the school term. [Regulation IV]

### C. Local government and county SB40 boards

1. The state closed new DD waiver enrollment on 15 October 2026 with no end date. Families in [county] who qualify now will look to local funders first. [Memo]
2. Please fund bridge supports (respite, short-term in-home help, service coordination) for people on the state wait list, and report how many people they serve.
3. Most adults with IDD under 63 who are not on a waiver have no paid respite route. DHSS respite starts at 63, and the Area Agency on Aging program needs a parent 55 or older. [HCBS Manual 3.50; DHSS AAA page]
4. Hands-on application help works: in a large trial, enrollment rose from 6% with no contact to 18% with outreach and help. Please fund a regional navigator service. Boards that provide DD support coordination can bill MO HealthNet Targeted Case Management for DD-eligible members. [Finkelstein and Notowidigdo; FY27 DMH book]
5. A county SB40 levy can be up to four mills (40 cents per $100) with voter approval. The county commission appoints all nine board members. Please appoint parents and self-advocates. [RSMo 205.970, 205.971]
6. Please adopt and publish a written appeal process for board funding decisions. State law gives none. [RSMo 205.968]
7. Transit: Access Express costs $2.00 a ride and runs only during fixed-route hours. Please keep fares and hours stable in the FY2028 city budget. [City Utilities]
8. Housing: HUD gives the public 45 days to review a housing agency plan before its hearing. Please adopt a disability preference and seek more Mainstream vouchers. [24 CFR 903.17; HUD table]

### D. Providers and nonprofits

1. Plan for no new waiver starts. Current participants continue; new enrollment is wait-listed with no end date. [Memo]
2. Submit the ISP, budget, and PON for every eligible person now, in writing, so the wait list date starts. Ask for a new PON after any change. [9 CSR 45-2]
3. Request a DHSS home care assessment for each waiting adult who has MO HealthNet. MO HealthNet must be active on the referral date. [HCBS Manual 4.05]
4. Count what you see: people referred who cannot start services, by reason (wait list, no staff, transport, cost, language), plus staff vacancies and wages. Publish totals only, with no count under 11.
5. Speak with one voice. County DD boards' 2026 priorities are "Safeguard Property Taxes," "Eliminate the Waiver Waitlist," and "Build Sustainable Solutions." [MACDDS]
6. Expect pressure on FY2028 rates: the state projects a shortfall of more than $500 million. [Governor, 30 June 2026]

## 6. Rules for nonprofits and key dates

**Rules.**
1. A 501(c)(3) must never support or oppose a candidate. Nonpartisan voter education is allowed.
2. Lobbying is allowed within limits. Consider the 501(h) election (IRS Form 5768). Budget bills, bills, ordinances, and ballot measures are "legislation." Comments to DMH, DSS, DHSS, MO HealthNet, or a housing agency plan are not lobbying under 501(h).
3. Staff who lobby as part of their job, are paid for it, are designated by the organization, or spend $50 or more a year on officials may need to register with the Missouri Ethics Commission by 5 January or within five days of starting ($10 fee). Testifying as a witness alone does not make a person a lobbyist.
4. No gifts to legislators or staff from a paid lobbyist or lobbyist principal.
5. Ask legal counsel to review these rules for your organization.

**Key dates.** 3 Nov 2026 election. Nov 2026 to early Jan 2027 Greene County budget meetings (2026 pattern). 1 Dec 2026 prefiling opens. 6 Jan 2027 session opens; confirm committee members. About 1 Feb 2027 first CDS moratorium term ends. About 5 Feb 2027 Governor's FY2028 budget. May 2027 Springfield city budget hearing (2025 pattern).
